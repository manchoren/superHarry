# Free-provider candidates

Binding provider rules and the current OpenRouter exception are in the [constitution](../../constitution.md#data-and-operations). The [simplified OpenRouter behavior](openrouter-simplification.md) is implemented locally; deployment and acceptance on the target VM remain open.

The current exception covers approved OpenRouter free text models subject to validated catalog metadata, user consent and the constitutional safeguards. The earlier Liquid-only restriction describes the previous decision and deployed test version. See [T179](../reference/backlog.md) for acceptance status and the VM deployment plan for operational status.

Provider terms, prices, models and account limits can change. Credentials stay outside the repository, and credential presence alone never admits a provider. Development admission is not release acceptance: T178 is administratively closed, while T179 and the remaining integration, recovery, quota, target-VM and release criteria remain open.

An earlier request reached Groq but returned a response that the then-current strict validator could not use; its outcome remains unknown and holds reservations for 1 request and 9,812 tokens. ## Shared controls and remaining limits

Provider order stays administrator-configurable. Every attempted route must satisfy admission, authenticated-user role, local protected-context preparation/integrity, applicable cost/privacy policy and safe-retry checks. The OpenRouter free-model exception accepts uncertain provider-side privacy/preservation and approximate token accounting, subject to the visible user warning, per-request checkbox, approximate quota opt-in and documented ceilings above. Unknown or partial outcomes retain their quota reservations and are never blindly repeated elsewhere. Local inference fallback is not yet available. Local selection has synthetic coverage; provider-side context conformance, durable provider reconciliation and target-VM validation remain open under T179.

Development credentials may be supplied locally through the ignored `.env` as `GROQ_API_KEY`, `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` or `GEMINI_API_KEY`; deployments use environment variables or an approved secrets facility. Never paste credential values into the repository, documentation, chat or validation output.
