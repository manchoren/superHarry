1. T002 and T014 are hard prerequisites for T016/T017; T003, T014 and T011 precede T018. T019 depends on T016, T017 and T014; T020 depends on T016, T017 and T014; T021 requires T018, T019 and T020. Design drafts may inform review, but unchecked prerequisites are not silently complete.
2. The direct list is not the transitive graph. A bounded resolver experiment needs explicit approval of its tools, command, isolation and egress under applicable gates; its output is a draft, not an approved lock or an authorization to install it.
3. Review the resulting exact graph and artifacts, including platform markers and ARM64 wheels or native builds, all mandatory scan coverage and failure behavior, then request G-ARCH for the specified dependency scope. Separately request G-OPS for an exact VM plan and G-SECURITY for configuration/secret decisions. Security, schema and contract approvals retain their own scope; production deployment is a separate decision.
4. Only after prerequisites and approvals, implement small scoped changes and tests, preserving user-visible English/Spanish catalogs where relevant. Execute the approved reproducible local checks and ARM64 smoke tests, record actual outcomes and limitations, and obtain the repository's required formal review. Never mark a design or an emulation-only result as hardware acceptance.
